119° NE (from Mecca)

الأَذَان

The Call to Prayer

The Azan (أَذَان) is the Islamic call to prayer, recited five times daily from mosques around the world.

اللهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللهُ أَكْبَرُ Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest ×2
أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ الله I bear witness that there is no god but Allah ×2
أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُوْلُ الله I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah ×2
حَيَّ عَلَى الصَّلاَة Come to prayer ×2
حَيَّ عَلَى الفَلاَح Come to success ×2

Prayer is Light

— Al-Bayhaqi & reported in various collections
